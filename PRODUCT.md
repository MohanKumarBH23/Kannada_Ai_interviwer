# PRODUCT.md — Kannada AI Interview & Hiring Platform

## 1. Vision & Purpose
A specialized, bilingual (Kannada/English) AI-driven recruitment and career advancement platform built specifically for Karnataka's workforce. The platform serves two primary personas:
- **Recruiters & Admins**: Automate screening, track campaign metrics, review AI candidate evaluations, and manage hiring pipelines efficiently.
- **Candidates (Job Seekers)**: Practice interviews with real-time AI feedback in Kannada and English, build professional profiles, and generate standardized PDF resumes.

---

## 2. Core Personas & Use Cases

### For Recruiters
- **Dashboard & Campaigns**: Monitor active roles, applicant flow, and screening completion rates.
- **AI Eligibility Screener**: Evaluate candidate suitability against structured job criteria and bulk-screen CSV applicants.
- **Communication Automation**: Trigger automated interview invitations via Telegram or SMS.

### For Candidates
- **AI Career & Profile Builder**: Input experience, education, skills, and headshot.
- **Instant Resume Generation**: Produce a clean, standardized PDF resume in one click.
- **Real-Time AI Interview**: Voice and text-guided interview sessions in Kannada and English with dynamic scoring and constructive feedback.

---

## 3. Product Constraints & Quality Floors
- **Bilingual Accessibility**: Kannada script requires increased line height (minimum 1.6) and careful vertical rhythm to prevent diacritic clipping.
- **Trust & Dignity**: Job seekers require an interface that conveys institutional trust, clarity, and fairness—avoiding gimmicky "futuristic AI" visual cliches.
- **Performance**: Instant loading and responsive layouts across entry-level smartphones, tablets, and desktop workstations.
- **Accessibility**: Strict WCAG AA contrast compliance across all text and interactive states.
